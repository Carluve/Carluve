## About me

PhD candidate in **Artificial Intelligence and Management** at Universidad de Alcalá, lecturer in postgraduate and executive programmes, and **Sr. Enterprise Account Executive at Cloudflare**, working with digital natives, startups and ISVs. My research studies the **economic and organisational impact of large language models**. Before Cloudflare I worked at Microsoft, Insight, Telefónica, Lenovo and Amazon Web Services.

- 🏫 Universidad de Alcalá — Spain · contributor to the **UAH × SISU Shanghai** programme
- 🔬 Research: LLM adoption in organisations, AI in education, medical education and LLMs
- ☁️ Everything I prototype ships on Cloudflare: Workers, Durable Objects, D1, R2, Workers AI
- 🧪 Vibe coding and agents as a daily workflow, not a demo
